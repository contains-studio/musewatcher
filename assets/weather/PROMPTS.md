# Clean weather artwork prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Each outfit was generated separately and visually inspected both at source size and after the production 72-pixel conversion. The first pass regenerated `mild-knit` from its previous artwork. Later passes used that clean result as the style reference and the corresponding previous outfit as the garment reference. These prompts are generation records, not instructions for the weather delivery skill.

Keep the [artwork notice](NOTICE.md) with copies.

## mild-knit

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Regenerate the SINGLE Muse character in reference image 1 as clean authentic low-resolution pixel art for a 72-pixel-tall game sprite. Reference 1 is the edit target and identity/outfit reference. Keep its round peach face, cream fluffy fur framing the face, black dot eyes with white catchlights, pink cheeks, small smiling mouth, squat cuddly proportions, frontal standing pose, arms relaxed beside torso, blue rugby knit with collar and buttons, olive gray shorts, cream shoes. Full body centered on pure solid black. Remove the floating cloud and floor shadow.
CRITICAL CORRECTION: completely remove the tiled grid and every thin lattice line covering the face, fur, and clothing. Pixel art means contiguous flat color clusters with crisp stepped silhouettes, NOT outlined square tiles. Face is one broad uninterrupted flat peach region, with only eyes, cheeks and smile. Fur is flat cream clusters with sparse larger tan shadow clusters at perimeter. Clothes use only 3-4 flat shades. No fabric texture, no mosaic, no embroidery grid, no graph-paper lattice, no dithering, no checkerboard, no hatching, no streaks or horizontal bands across the face. Do not preserve any of the source texture. Use an intentional coarse pixel grid equivalent to a roughly 56 by 72 pixel character, enlarged with nearest-neighbor crisp edges. No anti-aliasing or gradients. Keep existing identity, outfit, colors and general silhouette. No text or logos. Deliver just one full-body idle character, no sheet.
```
